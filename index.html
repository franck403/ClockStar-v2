Step 0 • No MicroPython Detected

We couldn't get an answer from MicroPython on this watch. That usually means one of two things:

Try this first:

  1. Unplug the watch completely and wait 10 seconds.
  2. If it has a reset or boot button, hold it while you plug the USB cable back in.
  3. Reconnect here and try again.

Still nothing? Choose "Install MicroPython (again)" anyway. With the BOOT button held while you plug the watch in, the chip's built-in bootloader lets us flash it even when no software works at all.

Step 0 • Flash MicroPython

Pick a MicroPython .bin file made for the ESP32-S3: a full image written at 0x0, like the CircuitMess "firmware.bin" or a "GENERIC_S3" build. This erases everything on the watch before the new firmware is written.
