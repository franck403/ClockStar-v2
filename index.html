Connect Your Watch

Plug the watch into your computer with a USB-C cable, click Connect, then choose the watch in the window your browser shows.

What would you like to do?

Install ClockStar

Copies the latest firmware to the watch, sets its clock from this computer, then restarts it.

Add-ons included: none

Installing replaces the firmware and files on your watch. Your settings (screen timeout, tilt to wake) are kept if the watch already has them.

Add-ons

Optional extras for your watch. Tick the ones you want, then install them here, or include them when you install ClockStar. No full reinstall needed.

Loading add-ons...

This only copies the add-on files, then restarts the watch. Add-ons you leave unticked are switched off, and the original buzzer driver comes back if you untick the Metronome.

Install MicroPython

Pick a MicroPython .bin file made for the ESP32-S3: a full image written at 0x0, like the CircuitMess "firmware.bin" or a "GENERIC_S3" build. This erases everything on the watch before the new firmware is written.
