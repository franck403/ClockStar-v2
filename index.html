Try this first:

  1. Unplug the watch completely and wait 10 seconds.
  2. If it has a reset or boot button, hold it while you plug the USB cable back in.

Still nothing? Choose "Install MicroPython (again)" anyway. With the BOOT button held while you plug the watch in, the chip's built-in bootloader lets us flash it even when no software works at all.

Connect Your Watch

Plug the watch into your computer with a USB-C cable, click Connect, then choose the watch in the window your browser shows.

Install ClockStar

Copies the latest firmware to the watch, sets its clock from this computer, then restarts it.

Add-ons included: none

Installing replaces the firmware and files on your watch. Your settings (screen timeout, tilt to wake) are kept if the watch already has them.

Add-ons

Optional extras. Tick the ones you want, then install them here, or include them when you install ClockStar.

Loading add-ons...

This only copies the add-on files, then restarts the watch. Add-ons you leave unticked are switched off, and the original buzzer driver comes back if you untick the Metronome.

Icons

Redraw the battery, phone, slider and the clock backgrounds, or import your own pictures. Changes are kept in this browser and also go along with a full install.

Changed icons: none

Install MicroPython

Pick a MicroPython .bin file for the ESP32-S3 (a full image written at 0x0). This erases everything on the watch.

We recommend MicroPython 1.29. It's the version we use, and it works best with ClockStar. Get the ESP32-S3 build on micropython.org.
